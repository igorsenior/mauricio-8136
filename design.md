# Mauricio Store — Design
Dark editorial outdoor commerce inspired by the provided reference, with original Portuguese content.
Palette: #141615 background, #1d201e panels, #ebece5 bone, #959b93 muted, #d2e7a7 pale lime accent.
Typography: Barlow Condensed bold monumental display, Poppins body. Uppercase tracked micro labels.
Layout: thin navigation, oversized type and floating realistic reel hero, asymmetrical spotlight, local product catalog, photographic story, editorial section, FAQ, spacious footer. Fine borders, restrained radii, generous negative space.
Frontend only: pages/store.tsx composed by pages/index.tsx, styles.css, local catalog data. No database, shop API, auth or real payments. Search, category filters, product details, cart quantity controls and localStorage persistence. Checkout is clearly illustrative.
Responsive: stacked editorial sections, two-column mobile catalog, accessible dialogs and drawer. Reduced motion respected.
